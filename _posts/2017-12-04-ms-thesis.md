The code behind my master's thesis at METU: a pipeline that detects
cyber-security events in Turkish, a language with no labelled security corpus to
train on.

It collects the raw material first. Selenium and BeautifulSoup scrapers pull
Turkish newspaper archives, and a separate collector reads the Twitter stream,
which is where an incident usually surfaces before it surfaces officially. On
top of that sits the detection work: a keyword vector learned from an incident
whose answer is already known, normalisation for Turkish morphology (one stem
becomes dozens of surface forms, and a bag of words treats them as unrelated
tokens), and a per-entity anomaly test rather than a sentiment classifier.

The method and its measured results are written up in the thesis and the ICISSP
paper: [Automatic Detection of Cyber Security Events from Turkish Twitter Stream
and Turkish Newspaper Data](/publication/2019-metu-masters-thesis). There is also
an [animated walk through the argument](/lab/cyber-events/) in the lab.
