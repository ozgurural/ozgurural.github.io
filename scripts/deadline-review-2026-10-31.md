## Current creative rule

Influences stay in the working research. The viewer receives an original AI
story: no film titles, character names, announced inspirations, recognisable
dialogue, reference labels or explicit character-to-component mappings.
The same rule applies to narration, captions, cover, page metadata, page copy
and the LinkedIn draft. Similarities are left for viewers to discover.

## Argument and scenes

A model has a proposal; the execution system controls the conditions for use.
The argument progresses through consequences rather than named analogies:

1. A proposal arrives after a finite opportunity closes.
2. Waiting for inference causes other work to queue.
3. A bounded handoff removes that waiting dependency.
4. A new arrival can carry an old observation and mismatched context.
5. Admission remains conditional and does not establish action safety.
6. Separate workers still share a resource budget; contention breaks timing.
7. Resource isolation and worst-case timing require their own evidence.

The existing door, clock, schedule hand, queue, model, mailbox, snapshot,
admission gate and resource-base sets remain. The camera exposes the dependency
under the workers. In the shared-resource scene, the counterexample and
isolation start on their spoken words, using measured word boundaries and
the narration warp. There are no decorative glitches, code rain or shake.

The isolation transition replaces the common surface with two contiguous
surfaces before separating them, so coplanar layers cannot produce the
depth flicker corrected in the earlier version.

## Scientific boundaries

The cinema is an authored schematic event schedule. It is distinct from the
unchanged interactive model: 360 controller jobs over six seconds at 60 Hz,
with chosen control and admission costs, observation age, context, outage and
injected shared stalls. Neither is a robot benchmark or certification result.

The cinema's readouts and objects use the same relevant event state. The
timing benefit depends on a bounded handoff and the illustrated resource
assumptions. Freshness and matching context are conditions for consideration,
not proof of a safe action. Plant behaviour, task success, the safety of the
baseline and the usefulness of AI proposals are not measured.

Narration source fields point to the actual experiment and its assumptions.
They do not use fictional events as scientific evidence.

must be rendered from the rebuilt timeline. Do not reuse an old render solely
because its duration is similar. The final delivery record contains actual
dimensions, duration, input/output hashes, measured loudness/peak and complete
decode results; technical checks do not certify a subjective artistic ranking.

iframe geometry and mocked portrait fullscreen. They also reject direct
reference names in narration, technical source fields, all on-screen cues,
film metadata and the lab copy. Browser emulation is not physical-device testing.
