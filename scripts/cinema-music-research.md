# Cinema music research

Research updated 30 October 2026. Companion to the
[storytelling runbook](cinema-storytelling-runbook.md).

## Brief and current status

Explore the tension, piano, bass and beat-led progression associated with the
owner's Clubbed to Death reference. Music should support the reasoning and
leave the narration intelligible. A dramatic arrangement can accompany an
accurate argument; it cannot manufacture scientific evidence or replace one.

The Model Heist configuration still uses
`dist/music/mixkit-587-discover.mp3` in
`scripts/cinema/model-heist.voice.json`. At the research-only snapshot on
30 October, that was the only source in `dist/music/`; no replacement was
downloaded, purchased, mixed or applied in that pass. The later authorized
Deadline implementation uses asset 149, as recorded below. No creator was contacted.

The entries below were screened using track-owner/catalogue descriptions and
licensing pages. They have **not** been auditioned against our narration.
Ranking describes the order to test, not a verified sonic match or a final
selection. Durations and tempos are catalogue values, not our measurements.

## Licensing facts to retain with a chosen track

These are the providers' published terms as checked for this research, not
a purchased licence certificate or a promise that automated claims cannot
occur. Recheck the selected track and intended distribution before use.

- **Bensound:** [Individual pay-per-track and subscription options][bensound-license]
  cover social-media videos. A generic free-download button is not the chosen
  project's licence record. Free attribution availability for Sleepless was
  not established in this pass.
- **Motion Array:** its [licence page][motion-license] includes social-media
  marketing for paid members and preserves coverage for projects finished
  while membership was active. It notes that music can have Content ID/P.R.O.
  registration. No subscription or clearance was obtained here.
- **Simon Wilkinson:** Dark Roads is offered as an individually licensed track,
  not as a reusable royalty-free collection purchase. The [licensing page][blue-license]
  lists separate non-commercial and commercial options, each for one unique
  production. A listening-only MP3 purchase grants no video-use licence.
- **Mixkit:** [the music information page][mixkit-music] permits social media
  under its free music licence, with no required attribution. Retain the
  [applicable licence][mixkit-license] with the asset record if selected.
- **Incompetech:** The Complex's track record identifies CC BY 4.0. Follow
  the [licence requirements][cc-by] for attribution and identifying edits.
- **TunePocket:** the track page permits social-media projects under its
  licensing terms, identifies AdRev Content ID registration, and says the
  free preview is evaluation-only. A preview is not a publication-ready asset.

No current prices are quoted here: the exact plan, end-user and distribution
conditions matter more than a potentially stale headline price.

## What to compare in a later private audition

Use identical narration, picture and perceived programme loudness across
candidates. Compare the opening, the mechanism reveal, the counterexample,
and the ending; do not choose only by the first ten seconds of an intro.

| Criterion | Listen or inspect for |
| --- | --- |
| Speech | Can every technical phrase be understood on a phone speaker at a normal level? |
| Dramatic fit | Does the music support discovery, uncertainty and a change of explanation without implying a larger result? |
| Structure | Can a complete musical phrase resolve near the actual ending? Does a useful build occur within the film? |
| Density | Do piano attacks, strings or drums mask the narrator? Would stems or a calmer edit solve it? |
| Evidence | Record which passages were auditioned and what was heard. Catalogue adjectives and waveform measurements are not listening evidence. |

Keep the current cue as a baseline. If none of the replacements improves the
film with narration, retain it. An original commissioned cue is another
possible route, not an action authorized by this note: a brief could request
restrained minor-key piano, an original breakbeat/bass figure, room for voice,
and a composed ending. Do not commission or purchase anything from this file
alone, or copy the reference melody/recording as a shortcut.

Once a replacement is authorized and selected, use the existing pipeline
in `CLAUDE.md`: retain the asset/licence, inspect its musical structure with
`scripts/music-map.py`, choose phrase boundaries, regenerate the mix with
the existing narration, and verify the final MP4s and delivery hashes. A
successful music-map analysis does not establish that a splice sounds right.
